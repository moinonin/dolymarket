# Roles and permissions

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Visitor** | Browse markets, order books, tickers, and live market feeds. |
| **Trader** | Deposit/withdraw USDC, place/cancel orders, trade AMM pools, redeem payouts, dispute resolutions (`100 AGX`), and follow Pro Desks. |
| **Community Creator** | Launch binary Community Markets with `$10+ USDC` initial pool liquidity and optional automated price-feed resolution. |
| **Pro Desk Operator** | Manage a public Pro Desk strategy that traders can copy-follow. |

## Account Rules
- You can view, cancel, or redeem **only your own** orders, positions, and trades.
- Private portfolio, order, and staking actions require signing in.
