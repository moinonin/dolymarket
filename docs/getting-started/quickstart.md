# Quickstart: your first trade in 5 minutes

## 1. Create an account

Choose one:

- **Email + password** (min 8 chars).
- **Passkey** (Face ID / Touch ID / security key) — no password needed.
- **Federated** — Google, Microsoft or Apple.

## 2. Fund your account

Deposit USDC from the cash ledger. Your balance is split into:

- **Available** — free to spend.
- **Locked** — reserved by open orders.

## 3. Open a market

Browse **Markets**, filter by category or status, and open an event. Each
market has one or more outcome tokens (e.g. YES / NO).

## 4. Read the book

- **Best bid / best ask** — the tightest prices on each side.
- **Mid price** — `(bid + ask) / 2`, a fair-value proxy.
- **Depth ladder** — resting size at each price level.

## 5. Place an order

Pick a side (Buy / Sell), a price, an amount (shares), and a
[time-in-force](../trading/order-lifecycle.md):

- **GTC** — rests in the book until filled or cancelled.
- **IOC** — fill what you can immediately, cancel the rest.
- **FOK** — fill the whole amount immediately or cancel.

Set **Post-only** if you never want to pay the taker fee.

## 6. Watch it fill

Fills are matched price-time priority. You'll see the order move from
`OPEN` → `PARTIALLY_FILLED` → `FILLED`, and your position updates.

## 7. Resolve and redeem

When the market resolves, winning tokens redeem at **$1.00 per share**. Claim
the payout from your portfolio.

## Hotkeys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `B` | Buy |
| `S` | Sell |
| `Enter` | Submit |
| `Esc` | Close modal / drawer |
