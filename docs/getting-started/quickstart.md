# Quickstart: your first trade in 5 minutes

## 1. Sign in or create an account

Choose one:

- **Web3 Crypto Wallet** — connect MetaMask or any EVM wallet and sign a one-time message (non-custodial, no password needed).
- **Passkey** — sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, or a hardware security key (register passkeys anytime under **Portfolio → Security**).
- **Email + password** — standard email sign-up (minimum 8 characters).

## 2. Fund your account

Deposit USDC in **Portfolio**. Your balance is split into:

- **Available** — free to trade or provide liquidity.
- **Locked** — reserved by open limit orders.

## 3. Open a market

Browse **Live Markets** for real-time global markets, or switch to the **Community** sub-tab (`Live Markets → Community`) to trade or create community binary markets.

## 4. Read the book

- **Best bid / best ask** — tightest prices on each side.
- **Mid price** — `(bid + ask) / 2`, a fair-value proxy.
- **Depth ladder** — resting size at each price level.

## 5. Place an order

Pick a side (**Buy** / **Sell**), price, share amount, and [time-in-force](../trading/order-lifecycle.md):

- **GTC** — rests in the order book until filled or cancelled.
- **IOC** — fills what it can immediately, cancels the rest.
- **FOK** — fills the entire size immediately or cancels.

Enable **Post-only** to avoid paying taker fees, or use **AMM & LP Pool →** on local/community markets for instant pool liquidity.

## 6. Watch it fill

Orders match by price-time priority (`OPEN` → `PARTIALLY_FILLED` → `FILLED`) and your **Portfolio** updates live.

## 7. Resolve and redeem

When a market resolves, winning shares redeem at **$1.00 USDC** each from **Portfolio** (and liquidity providers can claim remaining pool equity + fees via **Redeem LP**).

## Hotkeys

| Key | Action |

| `B` | Buy |
| `S` | Sell |
| `Enter` | Submit |
| `Esc` | Close modal / drawer |
