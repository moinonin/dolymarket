# Real-time data

AlgoXen streams live market updates directly to your browser:

- **Order books & prices**: Best bid, best ask, depth ladder, and recent trades update in real time without manual refreshing.
- **Your orders & fills**: Order status transitions (`OPEN` → `PARTIALLY_FILLED` → `FILLED`) and portfolio balances refresh automatically.
- **Automatic reconnect**: If your connection drops temporarily, the app reconnects automatically and resyncs the latest order book snapshot.
