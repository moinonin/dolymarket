# Do's and don'ts

## Do
- Use **Limit** orders (with **Post-only**) to control your exact entry price and avoid crossing wide spreads.
- Set a price you believe is below the true probability before buying.
- Cancel stale open orders; USDC or shares remain locked while they rest.
- Diversify across uncorrelated markets.
- Review the [Fees](trading/fees.md) formula and [Oracle Clarity](markets/market-details.md#2-oracle-clarity-score) score before trading.

## Don't
- Don't chase a price that has already moved sharply.
- Don't size a single position beyond your risk budget.
- Don't assume past Pro Desk performance predicts future results.
- Don't share your wallet seed phrase, account password, or passkeys.
- Don't treat this simulation as financial advice or a regulated exchange.
