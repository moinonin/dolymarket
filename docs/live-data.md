# Live market data

AlgoXen displays **live** prediction markets — real-time quotes, order books, price history charts, candlesticks, and trade tapes — under **Live Markets** in the top navigation.

## Filtering Markets

Use the filter controls on **Live Markets** to switch between:
- **Active** — currently open markets accepting orders.
- **Resolved** — settled historical markets showing the winning outcome.
- **Older →** — page back through historical resolved markets.

## How Winners Are Displayed

When a live market settles, the winning outcome settles at `100¢` (`$1.00`) and losing outcomes settle at `0¢` (`$0.00`). Resolved market cards highlight the winning outcome automatically.
