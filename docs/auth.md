# Authentication

AlgoXen supports three sign-in methods:

- **Web3 Crypto Wallet (SIWE)** — connect MetaMask or any compatible EVM wallet and sign a verification message. No password required.
- **Passkeys** — biometric or hardware key sign-in (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, YubiKey). Register or manage passkeys under **Portfolio → Security** after signing in with Email or Wallet.
- **Email + Password** — classic account registration with a minimum 8-character password.

See [Collateral & Wallet](portfolio/collateral.md) for managing your connected EVM wallet.
