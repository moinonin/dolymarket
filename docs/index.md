# AlgoXen User Guide

AlgoXen is a prediction market platform combining a **Central Limit Order Book (CLOB)**, an **LMSR Automated Market Maker (AMM)**, **1:1 USDC winning share settlement**, **Pro Desk copy trading**, and **non-custodial Web3 wallet signing**.

---

## Platform Navigation

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Live Markets** | Real-time global prediction markets, plus **Community** (local & user-created binary markets) and **Tags** sub-tabs. |
| **Desks** | Pro Desk leaderboard, track records, copy-follow controls, and **Copy Activity** sub-tab. |
| **Portfolio** | Balances, open positions, order history, plus **Trades** and **Settled** sub-tabs, and **Security** (passkeys). |
| **AGX** | AGX staking tiers, trading fee discounts, 24-hour reward epochs, and dispute staking. |
| **Resolution** | Active 2-hour oracle challenge windows, verifiable price proofs, and 100 AGX counter-bond disputes. |

---

## Getting Started

1. **Sign In** via **Web3 Wallet**, **Passkey**, or **Email + Password**.
2. **Fund Collateral** from **Portfolio** (simulation USDC deposit or connected EVM wallet).
3. **Trade** on any market using **Market**, **Limit**, or **Smart Route**, or open **AMM & LP Pool →** on local/community markets to trade against or fund the liquidity pool.
4. **Manage & Redeem**: Track fills and P&L in **Portfolio**, then redeem winning shares at **$1.00 USDC** each once resolved.
